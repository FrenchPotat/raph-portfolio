About Me

How I work, what I care about, and what drives the way I build products.

What I Do

I’m a Product Manager with a simple goal: make complex decisions and experiences feel easy.

At Verivox, that means helping hundreds of thousands of people navigate life’s not-so-fun-but-very-important stuff, like selecting the right utility contract. Our mission is to save our users time and money, and that’s what motivates my team and me every day.

I work on improving digital journeys through product discovery, experimentation, and data-driven decisions. Whether it’s designing better user flows, running A/B tests, or bringing a powerful customer insight into the room, I focus on making products that solve real problems and create measurable impact.

How I Work

My biggest strength is empathy.

I spend a lot of time understanding customers: their needs, frustrations, and motivations. From there, I translate insights into hypotheses, experiments, and product improvements.

I’ve worked on both 0→1 and mature product platforms, and I enjoy the different challenges each brings: exploration, iteration, and scaling what works.

I’m particularly curious about emerging technologies, AI and enjoy building small prototypes or automations to explore ideas quickly.

I’m also a strong communicator and facilitator. And yes, my French accent usually makes workshops and meetings slightly more entertaining.

Languages I Speak

I'm fluent in French, German, English, and conversational in Russian, Italian, and Spanish.

Fun fact: I actually learned Russian through a ‑day Duolingo streak (and counting 🦉).

Language for me is not just a tool, it’s how I build empathy and understanding across cultures.

Life Outside of Work

Outside of product work, I spend a lot of time with my French Bulldog, Sarah.

I enjoy calisthenics, specialty coffee, and plants, my apartment has slowly turned into a small urban jungle.

I’m also passionate about personal finance, watches, and technology, and I like exploring new tools or building small side projects just for fun.

What's Next?

My long-term goal is to grow into product leadership and mentoring, helping other PMs level up while building great products.

I’m particularly interested in the future of learning and education, and I’d love to contribute to products that help people grow and unlock opportunities.

Whether that means joining a mission-driven team or eventually building something of my own, my ambition is simple: create tools that make knowledge and opportunity more accessible.
